# Return & Refund Policy

## Return & Exchange Eligibility

Please note that all products purchased from our website are not eligible for return or exchange. We request customers to review product details carefully before placing an order.

---

## Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Product

In the unlikely event that you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product, you must notify us within **24 hours of delivery**.

To initiate a resolution process, please ensure the following:
- A clear unboxing video (recorded from the moment the package is opened)
- Clear photographs of the product showing the issue
- Images of the original packaging

Kindly email all required evidence along with your order details to:

**customercare@afterhues.com**

Our team will review your case and take appropriate action at the earliest.

---

## Failed Online Transactions

For unsuccessful or failed online transactions, the refund will be automatically processed to the original source account within **7 working days**.
