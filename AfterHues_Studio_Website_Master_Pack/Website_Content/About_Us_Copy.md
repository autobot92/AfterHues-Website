## About AfterHues Studio
At AfterHues Studio, we believe fragrance can shape the feeling of a space. Our candle collections are designed around different moods and moments—from soft florals and comforting gourmand notes to deeper woods and calm, spa-inspired blends.

Our hand-poured soy-wax candles bring together considered fragrance combinations and clean, understated packaging. Whether you are creating a quiet evening ritual, refreshing your workspace, or choosing a thoughtful gift, our collections make it easier to explore a scent that fits the moment.
